# CateringMS onboarding walkthrough

Demo company: Cedar & Sage Catering. This cut starts after the platform team creates the workspace. It follows the company administrator through the real setup screens. The demo uses fictional contact details and does not connect a payment account or import live customer data.

| Time | Screen | Onscreen takeaway | Voiceover |

| 00:00 | Meet Cedar & Sage | Cape Town caterer, first corporate lunch quote | Meet Cedar and Sage, a small caterer in Cape Town preparing its first corporate lunch quote. Alex, the operations manager, has a new CateringMS workspace. Follow along as Alex sets up the company information, chooses how customers will pay, and gets the team ready for that first quote. |
| 00:20 | Business details | Use the contact inbox your team monitors | On the Business screen, Alex checks the business name and contact email, then enters Cedar and Sage Catering as the trading name. These details appear on customer documents and emails, so use the inbox your team regularly checks. Select Save and continue. |
| 00:38 | Kitchen address | Dispatch starts at the kitchen | For our corporate lunch, the driver leaves from Cedar and Sage's kitchen in Cape Town. Alex enters that kitchen address here, rather than the client's venue. CateringMS uses the dispatch address as the starting point for delivery distances and route planning. |
| 00:55 | Branding | Forest green and copper | Cedar and Sage uses forest green and copper. Alex enters those as the primary and secondary colours and checks the preview. If your logo is hosted online, add its direct image link. This branding carries through to the customer portal and customer documents. |
| 01:13 | Bank transfers | Customers use the invoice number as the reference | The corporate client may prefer a bank transfer. Leave this blank if you don't accept transfers. |

| 02:04 | Email sender | Start with the shared sender | Cedar and Sage hasn't verified its own email domain yet, so Alex chooses the shared CateringMS sender. Clients can reply to the company's contact email. If you want quotes to come from your own domain, you can set it up and verify it later. |

## Production notes

The lesson follows one example company from the first setup screen to the dashboard. Each chapter pairs one decision or action with its actual screen and a short takeaway. The MP4 has chapter markers so viewers can return to a step. The lesson uses fictional business details and reenacts decisions without connecting a live payment provider or importing contacts.

The chapter structure and onscreen cues draw on [Ibrahim et al., Effects of segmenting, signalling, and weeding on learning from educational video](https://doi.org/10.1080/17439884.2011.585993).
