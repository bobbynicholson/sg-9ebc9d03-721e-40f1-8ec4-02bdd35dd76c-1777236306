# CateringMS onboarding walkthrough

Demo company: Cedar & Sage Catering. This cut starts after the platform team creates the workspace. It follows the company administrator through the real setup screens. The demo uses fictional contact details and does not connect a payment account or import live customer data.

| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00 | Welcome | Let’s set up a new CateringMS workspace. Here’s how Cedar and Sage Catering gets ready to take its first booking. |
| 00:07 | Business details | Start with your company name and contact details. Add your legal and registration details if they apply. |
| 00:14 | Kitchen address | Enter your kitchen or dispatch address so your team has the right starting point for delivery distances and routing. |
| 00:22 | Branding | Add your logo and brand colours. This gives the customer portal your company’s look. |
| 00:27 | Banking | If you accept bank transfers, add your business banking details for EFT. You can also come back to this later. |
| 00:36 | Online payments | If you take online payments, connect your company’s own payment provider. You can skip this while you’re getting set up. |
| 00:43 | VAT | Set whether you’re VAT registered so CateringMS can use the appropriate invoice heading. |
| 00:49 | Email sender | Use the shared CateringMS sender to get started, or set up and verify your own domain. |
| 00:56 | Alerts | Choose which alerts your team needs, and set up push notifications on the device you want to use. |
| 01:02 | Reminders | Review payment due dates and reminders, then decide which automatic emails to turn on. |
| 01:08 | Clients | Already have a customer list? You can import your contacts in bulk or add them later. |
| 01:13 | Finish | Select Finish setup and go to dashboard. Optional integrations like WhatsApp can wait until you’re ready. |
| 01:21 | Dashboard | Your workspace is ready for the next step. Use the dashboard checklist to keep setting up your team and get ready for your first quote. |
