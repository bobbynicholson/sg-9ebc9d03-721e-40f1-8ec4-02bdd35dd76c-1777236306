# CateringMS onboarding walkthrough

Demo company: Cedar & Sage Catering. This cut starts after the platform team creates the workspace. It follows the company administrator through the real setup screens. The demo uses fictional contact details and does not connect a payment account or import live customer data.

| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00 | Welcome | Welcome to CateringMS. This walkthrough follows Cedar and Sage Catering as it prepares a new workspace for its first quote. |
| 00:09 | Business details | Under Business, confirm the company name and contact email. Add your legal or trading name and any registration details you want to include on business documents. Select Save and continue. |
| 00:23 | Kitchen address | Enter the address your team dispatches from. CateringMS uses this location to calculate delivery distances and plan routes. Check the address before saving. |
| 00:35 | Branding | Choose your primary and secondary colours. Use the preview to check how they look, and add your company logo for the customer portal. |
| 00:43 | Banking | If you accept bank transfers, enter your business banking details. Customers who choose EFT will see the payment instructions and the invoice reference. |
| 00:54 | Online payments | To accept online payments, connect your company's payment provider. If your account is still being set up, select Skip for now and return to this step later. |
| 01:06 | VAT | Confirm whether your business is registered for VAT. This setting determines the heading on your invoices. If you are registered, enter your VAT number. |
| 01:16 | Email sender | Choose the address your quotes and invoices are sent from. You can use the shared CateringMS sender while your own domain is being verified. |
| 01:25 | Alerts | Select the events you want to receive alerts for. Enable push notifications on each phone or tablet where you want those alerts to appear. |
| 01:35 | Reminders | Set when payment is due, and decide whether to send a payment reminder. Review which other customer emails should go out automatically before saving. |
| 01:44 | Clients | If you already have a client list, use the bulk import to bring those contacts into CateringMS. If you are starting fresh, you can add clients as you go. |
| 01:55 | Finish | Review your setup, then select Finish setup and go to dashboard. You can return to Settings later to make changes or connect optional services such as WhatsApp. |
| 02:06 | Dashboard | From the dashboard, use the checklist to invite your team and add your menu and prices. Once those are in place, you can start preparing your first quote. |
