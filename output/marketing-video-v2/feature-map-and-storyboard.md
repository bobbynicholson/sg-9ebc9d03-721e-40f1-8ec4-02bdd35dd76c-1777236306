# Spit Braai Delivery - compact video pair

Two clear story cuts, both based on the current Spit Braai Delivery red-to-purple interface palette, authentic product screens, and illustrative live-action stock footage. The supplied kitchen view is shown during the order and dietary-requirements narration. No generated people are used.

- Full story: 72 seconds. Enquiry, quote, follow-ups, WhatsApp, invoice, kitchen order, team handoffs and event day.
- Short story: 43 seconds. Accepted order, booking update, kitchen requirements, crew handoffs and service.

## Creative direction

Warm, conversational South African English. Open on real braai footage; use a single clear product beat at a time; spotlight the exact UI detail being narrated; cut to the supplied real kitchen order view for dietary requirements; close with the Spit Braai Delivery name and a simple call to action. Keep titles concise and captions readable.

The stock clips are illustrative. The kitchen capture is from the client-supplied screenshot. Invoice and booking mock data remain fictional. Online payment is described only as available when a gateway is connected.
