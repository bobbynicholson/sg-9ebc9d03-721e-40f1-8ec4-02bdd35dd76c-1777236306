# Spit Braai Delivery video pair

Two concise promotional cuts replace the earlier long generic video. Both follow the booking-to-event story, use the current red-to-purple Spit Braai Delivery palette, and show the client-supplied kitchen order view while the narration mentions dietary requirements.

- Full story: 1:12. Enquiry and quote, email follow-ups, WhatsApp updates, invoice, kitchen prep, team handoffs and service.
- Short story: 0:43. Accepted order, booking change, kitchen requirements, crew handoff and event day.

Both videos are 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps with narration, music and burned-in captions. Companion SRT files are included. The kitchen screenshot is cropped to the actual Spit Braai Delivery app view, excluding the WhatsApp window and chat text.

Food and event footage is illustrative live-action stock from Pexels, used under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). It is not footage of the client or its customers. Booking and invoice records are fictional campaign examples. Online payment is described as available when a gateway is connected.
