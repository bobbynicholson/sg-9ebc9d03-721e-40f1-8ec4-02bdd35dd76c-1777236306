# CateringMS: two marketing cuts

Both edits follow one fictional catering booking from enquiry through service and close-out. The longer cut explains the connected workflow; the shorter cut keeps the core promise and the client-requested invoice and team-view visuals.

## Full Journey — about 3:02

**Story:** A company picnic request starts scattered across notes, a PDF, and chat. CateringMS keeps the event connected as the team quotes, plans, prepares, serves, and closes it out.

| Approx. time | Story beat and product proof |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:18 | Hook: one enquiry arrives with details in different places; establish one connected event. |
| 0:19–0:51 | Capture the lead and follow-up; build a menu quote; show the client review and acceptance; carry the accepted quote into an order. |
| 0:52–1:04 | Review bookings, open quotes, contribution estimates, workload, and configured capacity with the team. |
| 1:05–1:15 | Show the invoice document, deposit, balance, and payment options beside the invoice workspace. The added invoice preview is marked illustrative. |
| 1:16–1:36 | Keep WhatsApp replies with the booking and show configurable lifecycle-email timing and controls. |
| 1:37–1:55 | Review fields extracted from a PDF before creating a record; use the role-aware company knowledge assistant and its sources. |
| 1:56–2:24 | Move the confirmed event through kitchen prep and staffing, stock and shopping, then driver and delivery handoff. |
| 2:25–2:42 | Show kitchen, driver, and cleaning role dashboards together, plus installable PWA team access; close the event with equipment returns and readiness. |
| 2:43–3:02 | Resolve with the event in service, an owner dashboard for follow-ups and decisions, then the demo call to action. |

## Production notes

- Both files are captioned 1920×1080, 16:9 MP4s, using the existing product capture, narration, and catering footage.
- The invoice insert uses synthetic figures and says “Illustrative”; the online and EFT labels show payment options, not a completed gateway transaction.
- The role panels are genuine captured team screens. Nonessential demo-only values are softened in the panels.
- The assistant is presented as a staff knowledge tool with role-aware access, not as a client-facing chatbot.
- The OpenRouter Seedance 2.5 generation request returned **402 Payment Required**, so no new generated video footage could be added with the configured account. The edits use the existing captured material.
