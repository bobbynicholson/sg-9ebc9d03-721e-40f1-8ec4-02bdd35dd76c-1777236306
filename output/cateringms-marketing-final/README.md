# CateringMS marketing overview

A 65-second widescreen overview for catering businesses. It frames the core problem as event details getting scattered between the client conversation, quote, invoice, kitchen and delivery crew, then shows the booking carried through role-based workspaces.

## Story beats

- A braai booking begins with too many moving details.
- A quote becomes an order without re-entering the event.
- A service or dietary update stays beside the booking.
- The invoice makes the deposit and balance clear.
- Kitchen, driver and cleaning teams get the view for their work.
- CateringMS closes with ?Less admin. More catering.? and a demo call to action.

Spit Braai Delivery is the illustrative catering business in the product story. The user-supplied `1.mp4` provided the final CateringMS CTA. The app screens show booking, invoice and role-based team views. Food and event footage is illustrative stock. Booking and invoice examples are fictional; online payment is described conditionally on a connected payment gateway.
