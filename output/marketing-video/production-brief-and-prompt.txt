CATERINGMS — LESS ADMIN. MORE CATERING.
Production brief and generation prompt

Audience: Catering business owners, sales teams and operations managers.
Goal: Turn recognition of everyday admin problems into interest in a product demo.
Format: 1920 × 1080, widescreen, 30 fps; approximately 2 minutes 43 seconds.
Core promise: Keep the commercial booking connected from enquiry to invoice, with communication and operational context close at hand.

MASTER CREATIVE PROMPT
Create a polished marketing film for CateringMS with a natural two-person conversation: a busy catering owner asks practical questions, and a knowledgeable teammate explains how the platform helps. Begin with the pressure of scattered enquiries, repeated quote entry and payment follow-ups. Introduce the supplied CateringMS logo and the sequence Leads > Quotes > Orders > Invoices. Demonstrate the real application using illustrative customer data and Spit Braai Delivery branding. Give readable screen time to leads, quotes, order progress, invoice balances, Opportunity Lab, lifecycle emails, WhatsApp replies and the dashboard. Use close, purposeful screen movement and calm transitions. Explain benefits in everyday language. Describe Opportunity Lab as a planning aid using estimated contribution and configured capacity, with human review before committing. Describe emails and WhatsApp automation as configured workflows; preserve operator control. Use warm, professional ElevenLabs voices for the owner and teammate. Add understated original instrumental music and synchronised captions. Use Google Veo 3.1 for realistic South African catering footage. Composite exact logo and typography in the edit. Finish with the logo, Less admin. More catering., and a demo call to action at cateringms.com. Do not invent quantified time savings, revenue improvements or guaranteed results.

- Configured lifecycle email follow-ups and reminders.
