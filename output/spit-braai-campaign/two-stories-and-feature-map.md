# Spit Braai Delivery — two-film campaign

A fresh, fictional Constantia garden-event story built around CateringMS, with real product screens, Spit Braai brand styling, and generated food footage. All client, staff, invoice and booking records shown are synthetic.

## Full journey film

- **A full table starts with a clear plan.** — ILLUSTRATIVE STOCK FOOTAGE. A message lands on a Monday. A family is planning a summer celebration in Constantia: a spit braai for one hundred and twenty, on a date everyone will remember. One small change can travel a long way.
- **The detail that changes the day** — KEEP THE CONVERSATION IN CONTEXT. Lindiwe asks for twelve vegetarian plates and moves service to one. Spit Braai Delivery keeps that update beside the conversation, where the whole team can find it.
- **An enquiry becomes a clear brief** — ONE EVENT RECORD. The enquiry becomes a lead, with guest count, venue, date and dietary notes in one record. Add the next follow-up, so nothing depends on someone remembering the chat.
- **A menu made for this occasion** — BUILD AND SHARE. Now shape the menu for this event. Check quantities, price and timing, then send a quote Lindiwe can review on her phone.
- **Follow up on your terms** — EMAIL FOLLOW-UPS. Choose how email follow-ups work. Set the timing, edit the message and switch automation on for the bookings that need it, or off when they do not.
- **Accepted. Ready to plan.** — QUOTE TO ORDER. Lindiwe reviews and accepts. Convert the quote to an order, carrying the agreed details forward: one hundred and twenty guests, the garden venue, one o'clock service, and twelve separate plates.
- **The client can follow along** — A CLEAR CLIENT VIEW. From the client portal, she can check her booking, see what is next, and return to the same event details when she has a question.
- **An invoice with the event attached** — INVOICING AND PAYMENT. The invoice belongs to that booking too. Show the deposit, balance and due date clearly. If a payment provider is connected, the client can pay online; EFT and recorded payments can stay in the same trail.
- **Know what is paid and what remains** — FOLLOW THE PAYMENT STATUS. As a payment lands or is recorded, the amount due stays clear, and follow-up stays tied to the right event.
- **One event. Three useful views.** — INSTALLABLE PWA / ROLE-BASED WORKSPACES. Install the app on a phone as a progressive web app. The owner can check the event, while each crew member opens a view shaped around their own work.
- **The kitchen sees what is next** — KITCHEN DASHBOARD. In the kitchen view, the team sees the next event, guest numbers and prep work. The vegetarian request stays part of the same brief.
- **Plan the buying before prep** — STOCK AND ORDER DEMAND. Shopping can compare upcoming order demand with stock on hand before the supplier list is ready. Less last-minute guessing in the storeroom.
- **A clean handoff to the driver** — DISPATCH TO THE FIELD. Dispatch assigns the driver, checks route coverage, then hands off to a route view with the stop details and delivery checks.
- **Close the loop after service** — CLEANING DASHBOARD. After service, cleaning has a dedicated workspace for returns and inspections, so equipment can be washed, checked and made ready for the next event.
- **Ready before the first plate.** — ILLUSTRATIVE STOCK FOOTAGE. Then the food arrives hot, the carving station opens, and the hosts get to spend time with their guests. The work stays with the team; the moment stays with the people.
- **From first message to final plate** — CATERINGMS FOR SPIT BRAAI DELIVERY. Catering M S connects the enquiry, the kitchen and the last return. Spit Braai Delivery, ready from first message to final plate.

## Short event-day film

- **Before lunch, everyone needs the same plan.** — ILLUSTRATIVE STOCK FOOTAGE. A garden ready for lunch. One hundred and twenty guests. Before the first plate lands, the whole team needs the same plan.
- **One small change. Everyone in sync.** — WHATSAPP ACTIVITY WITH THE BOOKING. Then the host adds twelve vegetarian plates and moves service to one. The update stays with the booking, right where the team can act on it.
- **From a thoughtful quote to a confirmed order** — QUOTE / ACCEPT / ORDER. The menu is shaped around the occasion. When Lindiwe accepts, the quote becomes an order with the date, headcount, menu and service time ready for the next step.
- **A clear view for the client too** — CLIENT PORTAL / INVOICE / PAYMENT STATUS. Lindiwe can check the booking and invoice in her portal. When a payment provider is connected, online payment can join the same invoice record.
- **Three teams. The right view for each.** — PWA WORKSPACES FOR THE CREW. On a phone, the kitchen sees prep, the driver sees the route, and cleaning sees what must come back. Everyone has a useful view of the same event.
- **Dispatch takes it to the venue** — ASSIGN / HAND OFF / DELIVER. Dispatch connects the drop-off to a driver, then the cleaning team closes the equipment loop after service.
- **Let the moment take centre stage.** — ILLUSTRATIVE STOCK FOOTAGE. The chef can carve, the crew can serve, and Lindiwe can be with her guests instead of chasing the details.
- **One booking. Carried all the way through.** — CATERINGMS FOR SPIT BRAAI DELIVERY. Catering M S. One booking, carried all the way through. Spit Braai Delivery.

## Creative treatment

- Warm charcoal, ember and saffron palette; South African English narration; genuine live-action stock footage, with no generated people.
- Product captures use spotlight dimming, pulsing orange focus borders, a moving cursor, click cues, slow push-ins and concise kinetic titles.
- The long film traces enquiry → quote → client review → order → invoice/payment → team handoffs → service and returns. The short cut follows the event-day change through role-specific PWA views.
- Stock footage is illustrative. Invoices and all displayed business records are fictional campaign mock data. Online payment is described conditionally on a gateway being connected.
