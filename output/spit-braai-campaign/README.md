# Spit Braai Delivery campaign

Two original CateringMS marketing films follow one fictional 120 guest Constantia garden celebration. The edit uses product screen captures and licensed live-action food and event footage. No AI-generated character shots are used; human footage is illustrative stock.

## Films

- **Full journey (3:13):** enquiry, WhatsApp update, quote, optional email follow-ups, client review, order, invoice and payment tracking, PWA team views, kitchen preparation, shopping, dispatch, cleaning and event day.
- **Event-day cut (1:24):** a tighter story about a booking change, quote acceptance, client visibility and the kitchen, driver and cleaning handoffs.

Both exports are 1920 x 1080, 16:9, 30 fps, with narration, music, captions and companion SRT files. All client, staff, booking and invoice records shown are fictional campaign data. Online payment is described conditionally on a payment provider being connected.

The food and gathering footage is illustrative stock, not a recording of the client or its customers. The clips come from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and are used under its license. Sources: [outdoor barbecue](https://www.pexels.com/video/outdoor-barbecue-5210785/), [close-up barbecue](https://www.pexels.com/video/close-up-of-barbecue-12047777/), [finger food](https://www.pexels.com/video/people-getting-finger-food-from-the-table-6760602/), and [garden gathering](https://www.pexels.com/video/people-eating-at-a-party-8479331/).
