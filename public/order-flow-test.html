What to watch for (the things that usually break)

  • Notifications: at every handoff (confirm → kitchen, assign → driver, delivered → admin) confirm the right role actually got pinged. Silent handoffs are the known weak spot.
  • Status guards: driver delivery is blocked outside 24h of the event; confirm is blocked if the deposit is unpaid. Use a near-term event date so you don't hit these.
  • Equipment: if you skip adding equipment in Phase 1, the driver collection leg won't appear. Include at least one equipment item.
  • Slug query: if an admin page looks empty, you probably dropped the ?company_slug= param.
