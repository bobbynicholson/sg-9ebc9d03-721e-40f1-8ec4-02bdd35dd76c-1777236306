## 💰 Dealify & Deal Sites Strategy

### How to List on Dealify

**Process:**
1. **Research:** Verify Dealify accepts SaaS products (not all deal sites do)
2. **Prepare Offer:** Create exclusive deal (e.g., "Lifetime access for $99" or "3 months for $1")
3. **Submit:** Follow their vendor submission process
4. **Promote:** Cross-promote deal on your social media
5. **Support:** Respond quickly to deal site comments/questions

**Best Practices:**
- Time deals with marketing campaigns (launch, holidays)
- Offer genuine value (minimum 50% discount)
- Set clear terms (what's included, limitations)
- Collect emails for future marketing

**Alternative Deal Sites:**
- AppSumo (SaaS focused)
- StackSocial
- Mighty Deals
- PitchGround (lifetime deals)
- DealMirror
