
# Catering Management Platform - Strategic Feature Roadmap

## Executive Summary
This platform is designed to solve the core profitability challenges in the South African catering industry by:
- Reducing admin costs through automation
- Enabling owner-operated businesses to scale
- Connecting all stakeholders seamlessly
- Providing real-time visibility and control

## Core Features Implemented ✅

### 1. Lead & Quote Management
- Lead capture (manual + web integration ready)
- Professional quote generation with automated pricing
- Email automation for quotes and follow-ups
- Customizable email templates

### 2. Order & Event Management
- Calendar system with availability blocking
- Payment reconciliation and tracking
- Automated order workflow
- Multi-stakeholder visibility

### 3. Team Coordination
- Kitchen portal for prep schedules
- Shopping team portal for procurement
- Cleaning team equipment management
- Driver delivery coordination

### 4. Client Experience
- Real-time GPS tracking of deliveries
- Client portal for order management
- Complaint portal with 24hr response SLA
- Automated notifications at every stage

### 5. Equipment & Inventory
- Stock management with auto-alerts
- Equipment availability calculator
- Cleaning time accounting
- Rental item tracking
