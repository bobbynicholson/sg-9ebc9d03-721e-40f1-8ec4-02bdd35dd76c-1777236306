# Catering Management Platform - Pricing Strategy & Business Model

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive pricing strategy for our catering management platform, designed to attract South African catering businesses while ensuring long-term profitability and sustainable growth.

## Market Analysis

### Target Market: South African Catering Industry

**Market Characteristics:**
- Small to medium-sized catering businesses (10-500 events/year)
- High operational complexity with low margins (typically 8-15% net profit)
- Manual processes leading to high admin costs
- Owner-operated businesses due to margin constraints
- Limited access to affordable, integrated software solutions
- Growing demand for professional event services

**Pain Points We Solve:**
1. Manual quote generation and follow-ups (15+ hours/week)
2. Inventory management and waste (15-20% food waste typical)
3. Poor supplier price tracking (missing 8-12% savings opportunities)
4. Disconnected communication between teams
5. Equipment tracking and cleaning scheduling chaos
6. Driver and logistics coordination issues
7. Lack of after-sales engagement (losing 60% repeat business potential)

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks & Solutions

**1. Slow Customer Adoption**
- Solution: Aggressive early-bird discounts, free trials, case studies
- Pivot: Offer freemium tier if needed (limited features)

**2. High Churn**
- Solution: Excellent onboarding, proactive support, regular feature updates
- Measure: Monthly NPS surveys, usage analytics

**3. Infrastructure Costs Spike**
- Solution: Optimize database queries, implement caching, use serverless where possible
- Backup: Raise prices 10-15% after Year 1 (still massive value)

**4. Competition**
- Solution: Move fast, build moat with integrations, own customer relationships
- Defense: Patent key features (receipt scanning AI, expiry tracking algorithms)

**5. Economic Downturn**
- Solution: Catering is recession-resistant (people still have events)
- Diversify: Add features for corporate catering, meal prep businesses
