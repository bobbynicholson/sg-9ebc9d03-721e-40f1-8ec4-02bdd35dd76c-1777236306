# Product

## Register

product

## Users
CateringMS serves working operators inside a catering company: owners, company admins, sales staff, kitchen managers, kitchen staff, shopping staff, cleaning managers, cleaners, drivers, platform admins, and repeat clients. They use it during active work, often under time pressure, to understand what needs attention, who owns it, and what action is safe to take next.

## Product Purpose
CateringMS is the operating system for a catering business. It connects leads, quotes, orders, menus, inventory, shopping, kitchen prep, driver dispatch, equipment returns, cleaning, invoices, payments, staff shifts, and client self-service into one tenant-scoped workflow. Success means every role can open their portal and immediately know today's work, blockers, financial impact, and next action without hunting through unrelated pages.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, premium. The product should feel like a professional control room for live catering operations: clear enough for shift staff, strong enough for owners, and polished enough for client-facing portals.

## Anti-references
Do not make the app look like a generic SaaS demo, a purple-blue AI dashboard, a marketing landing page, or a pile of unrelated admin screens. Avoid gradient text, decorative glass panels, vague "Coming soon" pages, oversized hero sections inside authenticated portals, unclear table labels, and role names that change from page to page.

## Design Principles
- One operating language. Use the same nouns everywhere: orders, quotes, prep list, buy list, returns, washing, damages, invoices, payables, shifts, and notifications.
- Role-first clarity. Each portal should show what that role owns today, what is blocked, what is due next, and where the data came from.
- Premium restraint. Use quiet surfaces, strong alignment, readable type, subtle motion, and brand accents only for action, state, and selection.
- Data with context. Metrics need plain labels, time windows, owners, and empty states that explain how to create the missing data.
- Shared components over one-offs. Admin, client, kitchen, cleaning, shopping, driver, and platform pages should reuse the same shell, header, card, table, badge, loading, and empty-state vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast. Keep focus rings visible, controls keyboard accessible, icon buttons labelled, and touch targets at least 44px. Support reduced motion. Avoid colour-only status meaning by pairing colour with text, iconography, or both.
