## PRODUCTION TIPS

### Voiceover Direction
- **Professional but friendly** tone
- **Clear, confident delivery**
- **Emphasis** on key phrases: "all-in-one", "purpose-built", "real-time"
- **Pace:** Conversational but energetic (around 150 words per minute)

### On-Screen Text Guidelines
- **Large, readable fonts** (minimum 48pt)
- **Short phrases** (3-5 words maximum)
- **Appear and fade smoothly**
- **Don't compete with narration** - complement it

### Call-to-Action Best Practices
- **Make URL prominent** - large, centered
- **Include social proof** if available ("Join 500+ catering businesses")
- **Show actual interface** - build trust with real product
- **Multiple CTA options**: "Start Free Trial" + "Book a Demo"
